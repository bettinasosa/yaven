## Design requirements

Never add decorative marketing tags, badges, eyebrow labels, kickers, or
tagline strips at the top or bottom of heroes, sections, or pages. This rule
applies to all audience variants and redesigns. See
`.design-sync/conventions.md` and `site-script.md`. Functional product UI
labels are not decorative marketing tags.

## Brand spelling

Always write **yaven** in lowercase in visible copy, headings, buttons, metadata,
accessibility labels and design files, including at the start of a sentence.
Keep code identifiers unchanged.
