The Yaven 3D prism brand mark (cream / rainbow / navy), self-contained as an
inlined image — no asset URL or pipeline needed. `height` scales it (number =
px, or any CSS length); width follows the mark's natural aspect ratio. Works
on both the cream (`--cream`) and primary-blue (`--primary`) brand
backgrounds.

```tsx
<YavenLogo height={80} />
<YavenLogo height="3em" />
```
